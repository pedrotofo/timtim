# Exitus Kids: prompts para o Google Flow (Veo)

**Como usar:** no Flow, crie um projeto e suba como *Ingredients* as imagens da Tia Chris, do Tio Bento e de um aluno de uniforme (pasta `artes/`). Use **Ingredients to Video** nas cenas com personagens. Use **Frames to Video** quando quiser partir de uma tela pronta: a capa, o mapa ou uma das fases.

Os prompts estão em inglês porque o Veo segue melhor as instruções assim. As falas ficam em português. Formato: 16:9, 8 s por cena.

**Bloco de estilo:** cole no fim de todos os prompts.
> Polished 3D animated feature-film style, soft global illumination, vibrant sunny palette of navy blue, Christus red, sky blue, cyan and sunny yellow, family-friendly, no violence, smooth camera, high detail.

## Teaser de 40 s: "A Grande Expedição"

**Cena 1: Abertura (Frames to Video, partindo de `00-capa.png`)**
> Slow push-in on the game cover: Tia Chris points forward excitedly, Tio Bento waves, two 6-year-old kids in white school uniforms look at a glowing map of Ceará. Floating letters A B C and numbers 1 2 3 gently orbit in the sky. Sparkles burst as the 'EXITUS KIDS – A Grande Expedição' logo pops in.
> Narração (Tia Chris): "Oi, explorador! Pronto pra uma grande aventura?"

**Cena 2: Praia das Letras (Ingredients: Tia Chris + aluna)**
> On a sunny turquoise beach in Fortaleza with a jangada sailboat in the background, Tia Chris kneels next to a 6-year-old girl in a white school uniform with navy and red striped collar. Three big seashells in the sand show the letters B, D and P. The girl taps the shell with the letter B, it glows golden and flies into a sand castle, starfish confetti bursts. Tia Chris claps happily.
> Fala (Tia Chris): "B de bola! Muito bem!"

**Cena 3: Feira dos Números (Ingredients: Tio Bento + aluno)**
> At a colorful northeastern Brazilian street fair with bandeirinhas, Tio Bento stands behind a fruit stall holding a basket of cashew fruits (cajus). A 6-year-old boy in a white school uniform counts them, pointing one by one as glowing numbers 1 to 8 appear above each caju. Tio Bento gives a thumbs up and laughs.
> Fala (Tio Bento): "Oito cajus! Você é bom de conta!"

**Cena 4: Mapa e progresso (Frames to Video, partindo de `02-mapa.png`)**
> Camera glides over a stylized 3D diorama map of Ceará: beach, sea with sailboats, fair, sertão with cactus and goats, a lighthouse library. A dotted path lights up level by level, gold stars pop above each completed level, and a padlock opens on the next one.
> Narração: "São cinco mundos para explorar, do jeito de cada criança."

**Cena 5: Álbum de figurinhas (Frames to Video, partindo de `07-recompensa.png`)**
> A sticker album opens; a new round sticker of a sea turtle flies in and lands in its slot with a sparkle. Tia Chris jumps celebrating with confetti.
> Narração: "Cada conquista vira uma figurinha do Ceará."

**Cena 6: Para a escola e as famílias (Frames to Video, partindo de `08-painel-professor.png`)**
> Smooth push-in on a laptop showing a teacher dashboard with progress bars per skill and a colorful heatmap; numbers animate up. Cut to a parent smiling at a phone notification "Seu filho conquistou 3 estrelas hoje!".
> Narração: "Professores e famílias acompanham cada passo, alinhado à BNCC."

**Cena 7: Fecho (Ingredients: Tia Chris + Tio Bento)**
> Tia Chris and Tio Bento stand side by side in front of the Fortaleza seaside at golden hour, waving at the camera; the 'EXITUS KIDS' logo appears above them with sparkles.
> Fala (os dois): "Exitus Kids. Aprender é uma grande expedição!"

## Animações curtas para o jogo (loops de 4 a 6 s)

1. **Tia Chris acenando:** "Tia Chris waves hello and smiles, idle breathing, plain light-blue background, seamless loop."
2. **Tio Bento comemorando:** "Tio Bento raises both fists celebrating and laughs, confetti, plain light-blue background, seamless loop."
3. **Dica:** "Tio Bento raises a finger as a glowing light bulb appears above his head, plain light-blue background."
4. **Incentivo após erro:** "Tia Chris tilts her head kindly and gives an encouraging thumbs up, gentle, plain light-blue background." Fala: "Quase! Vamos tentar de novo?"
