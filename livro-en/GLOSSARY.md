# Van Gogh in Pencil: translation glossary

English edition of *Van Gogh a Lápis* (Pedro Torres). American English, plain and direct, the voice of a teacher who draws. The reader is a beginner.

## Book
- *Van Gogh a Lápis* → *Van Gogh in Pencil*
- "Teoria e prática para desenhar como Vincent" → "Theory and Practice for Drawing Like Vincent"
- "N exercícios em quatro níveis" → "N exercises in four levels"

## Style rules
- Dates: "Arles, agosto de 1888" → "Arles, August 1888". Ranges: "1881 a 1883" → "1881 to 1883" (no dashes in ranges).
- Decimals with a point: "44,5 × 27 cm" → "44.5 × 27 cm".
- Measurements: give inches first, then centimeters in parentheses where the reader has to cut or measure something ("8 × 12 in (20 × 30 cm)"). Artwork dimensions stay in centimeters.
- Letters: "(carta 639)" → "(letter 639)". Quotations from Van Gogh's letters: translate the Portuguese wording into plain English in your own words. Do not reproduce any published English translation from memory (those translations are copyrighted); the book credits the quotes as the author's own translations.
- No em dashes or en dashes in running text. Use a comma, colon, parentheses or a new sentence.
- Follow the humanizer guide (`/home/user/blader/humanizer/SKILL.md`): no inflated significance, no "not X but Y" when nothing is being corrected, no rule-of-three lists for rhythm, no staged rhetorical questions, no closing sentence that restates the paragraph, no sentences about the book itself.
- Keep every HTML tag, class, id, src, href and `<!-- id: ... -->` marker exactly as is. Translate text, `alt` attributes, figcaptions and the title field of the `<!-- id: x | Title | kind | ... -->` markers (the id and kind fields stay).
- Exercise codes (R01, F03, C12, A01, B04, P03...) stay the same.
